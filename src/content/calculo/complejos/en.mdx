<Section eyebrow={"06 · Polar form"} title={"Polar form ρ<sub>θ</sub>"} lead={"A point is also fixed by two pieces of data: how far it is from the origin and the angle it makes with the positive real axis. Like giving directions as “5 km, heading north-east”."}>
